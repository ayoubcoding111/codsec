Project Details · Cinematic Scroll Experience

Scroll-driven landing page for Jesko Jets private aviation — fly straight through an airplane window from cabin to open sky, then land on About, Fleet, Advantages and Booking. GSAP pin + scrub, Lenis smooth scroll, zero build step.

01 · Cinematic Hero & Window Zoom

Pinned zoom straight through the window

The hero pins on scroll and rushes the cabin layer 5.5× (4.2× on mobile) straight through the airplane window hole into open sky — headlines, brand label, clouds and vignette all choreographed to that one scrub. The window itself is a true radial-gradient CSS mask computed from the real image naturalWidth plus object-fit: cover math, so the hole stays pixel-locked through the whole zoom on any viewport.

  • GSAP ScrollTrigger pin + scrub, no autoplay jumps
  • fitMask() recomputed on resize + refresh — zero drift
  • Traveling brand label docks into the nav + sticky Book button
Hero — cabin window, scroll to fly through
02 · About, Fleet & Advantages

Statement reveals, Gulfstream cards, glass rows

About reveals its statement letter by letter tied to scroll, with alternating photo rows sliding in from their own side and a glass stats bar — 5K+ missions, 174 countries, Dubai base, 24/7 ops. Fleet spotlights the Gulfstream 650ER (11,263 km range, up to 12+1 cabin, 5.52 m³ baggage) in a responsive card grid, and Advantages pairs a sticky editorial intro with four glass rows over a single full-page sky photo.

  • Scrubbed character reveal, reduced-motion safe
  • Gulfstream 650ER range, cabin, baggage, charter cards
  • Sticky intro + glassmorphism over one sky background
Advantages — glass cards, sticky intro
03 · Booking & Contact

Demo booking funnel, Dubai base

Contact section combines a demo booking form (name, email, phone, route with confirmation note) with a Dubai contact card — phone, email, base, aircraft and hours — plus brand footer and back-to-top.

  • Labeled inputs, confirmation state, backend-ready hook
  • Dubai day/night photography, contact pills
  • Semantic landmarks, focus rings, alt text throughout
Booking — demo funnel, Dubai base
