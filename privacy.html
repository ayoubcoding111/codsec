Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. Short version: we only use your details to reply with a quote. No selling, no newsletter, no surprises.

1. What we collect

When you use the contact form, we ask for:

  • Full name — so we know who we are talking to.
  • Email address — so we can reply within 24 hours.
  • Project types — what you picked (e.g. Landing Page, E-Commerce Platform).
  • Add-ons — optional extras you ticked (e.g. Chatbot, Custom Domain).
  • Project details — anything you write in the message box.
  • Consent record — the fact you ticked “I agree to the privacy policy”, plus when.

We do not collect payments, passwords, or any data you do not type into the form.

2. Why we use it

  • To understand your project and prepare a fixed quote.
  • To contact you back about that quote (email only).
  • To improve the form if something is unclear (aggregated, never personal).

Legal basis: your consent (the checkbox) and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries.

3. What we never do

  • No selling or renting your data to anyone.
  • No newsletter or marketing emails — only replies to your request.
  • No third-party trackers or ad pixels on this site beyond the CDN libraries that render it.

4. Storage & retention

Right now the form is a demo — pressing submit does not send or store anything anywhere; your text stays in your browser. Once the backend is connected, submissions will be kept only as long as needed to handle your request (quotes + follow-ups), then deleted on request. We will document the exact retention period here when that goes live.

5. Your rights

You can ask at any time to see, correct, or delete what you sent us. To do that, reply to our quote email or contact us through the site and we will act promptly.

6. Cookies & local storage

This site itself sets no cookies. The contact form remembers nothing after you leave the page. Embedded libraries (fonts, video CDN) may apply their own standard technical handling — nothing we control or profile you with.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above changes too. Continued use of the form after that means you accept the new version.
